# Thoughtbox 1.0 beta 4

This update adds a privacy-conscious way to capture selected text from another app directly into your persistent Draft.

## What is new

- Use **Capture Selection** with Control–Option–Shift–Space to append the currently selected text to your Draft and continue writing in Thoughtbox.
- Configure Capture Selection independently from the existing quick-capture shortcut, including restoring either shortcut to its default.
- Review and manage Accessibility permission status from Settings. Thoughtbox asks for permission when you explicitly use Capture Selection or choose to open Accessibility Settings.
- Selection failures stay out of your way: the Draft remains unchanged and an accessible message explains what to do next.
- Selected text is handled by a narrowly scoped helper, is never logged by the helper, and remains stored locally in your Draft.

Thoughtbox is a free, unlisted beta for capturing Markdown Thoughts, organizing them into Projects, searching active collections, recovering work from Trash, and exporting portable Markdown.

Thoughtbox stores its library locally on this Mac. It has no account, analytics, advertising, sync service, or background upload of Thought content.

Thoughtbox includes Portable Markdown export and signed automatic updates through **Thoughtbox > Check for Updates…**.

## Beta limits

- Capture Selection depends on the selected app exposing its selection through macOS Accessibility. Some apps or content may not make selected text available.
- Images are represented by their alt text; image files are not loaded or exported.
- Import is not available in this beta.
- Sync between Macs is not available in this beta.
- The interface is currently English only.

Before installing a later build, use **Export > Export All…** to make a portable Markdown backup. **Export All excludes Thoughts in Trash, so restore any trashed Thoughts you need before exporting.** Thoughtbox requires macOS 14 or later and checks the signed Thoughtbox update channel for new beta builds.
